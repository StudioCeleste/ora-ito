mamo

An artistic creation space in the sky.

It is here, at the top of La Cité Radieuse built between 1945 and 1952, that the MAMO created by Ora ïto is located. Le Corbusier's masterpiece thus offers itself a new territory by becoming the setting and springboard for future creation.

“Could we find better? More powerful, more iconic, more exhilarating, more energetic than this roof terrace placed in the air?”

In this new exceptional art center, the Corbu spirit blows, impulses and diffuses. The Radiant City is not a monument, it is a manifesto. Concrete bar erected on stilts, baptized Housing Unit by Le Corbusier, its architect, renamed "the house of Fada" by the locals, known and celebrated by all lovers of architecture around the world.

This residence of 337 duplex apartments is a vertical village, with its indoor streets, restaurant, grocery store, bookstore, hotel, nursery and school. A concrete liner anchored in solid ground, the Radiant City is adorned with a roof terrace which is also a ship's deck, a lookout in the open sky. The view embraces the territory 360°.
