UFO

Category Prototype
Brand Citroën
Collection Limited edition
Year 2011

Ora ïto naturally imagined other modules to join the initial project. A continuity of imagination which allows us to multiply actions and give another thought to “Evomobil”. He created “UFO” which is inspired by an iconic Citroën car.

The DS model commonly used by the President is now inscribed in the "genetic transformations" originating from Ora ïto's artistic vision. The vehicle is based on the future of transportation mixed with one of the most famous cars in the world.

The UFO sculpture combines the dream with the formal, the future with technology. The idea of ​​working with multiple entities allows all “genetic transformations” to spread a universal message to the automotive industry, through artistic vision and new directions never before explored.
