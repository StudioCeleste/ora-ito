Idylle bottle

Category Cosmetic
Brand Guerlain
Collection Guerlain
Year 2009

Based on his idea of ​​new perspectives, his sense of the times, made of modernity and minimalism, the attraction to forms and functional rationalism, Ora ïto created the design of the bottle of the latest women's perfume from the house of Guerlain, Idylle. The encounter with a feminine perfume involves grip, ergonomics, gestures, elegance, fluidity and dynamism.

He created the form that would evoke all these worlds. A new curve is born, a look of our time, of dazzling simplicity and elegance. The bottle, curved and gold, futuristic, shines like a golden tear. Idylle is of course the woman, it is even the quintessence of the woman; it’s a drop of gold.
