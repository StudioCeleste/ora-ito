Venus lounge chair

CategoryPrototype
BrandCitco
CollectionLimited edition
Year 2017
Ora Itoand the Italian marble specialistCitcowill reveal the chairVenusat the Milan Furniture Fair next April. For 20 years, Ora Ito has been building a sensual and rational volume language adapted to each function. With Venus, he creates a sculptural chair like a chosen piece of his own DNA. A Venus with imperious vivacity, whose asymmetry causes spectacular nervous ruptures. The celebration of drawing in space by a visual designer who digitally sculpts marble centimeter by centimeter with incredible dynamic precision.

When the best of Italian craftsmanship meets the vitality of streamline.

The biomorphic, polished curves draw bridges, throw themselves cantilevered into the void, finally call out for a caress. The block of marble is grooved by a hollow joint which traces a rational line on the organic form, underlines its functionality, its ergonomics and domesticates its impetuosity. Naturally intended for gardens and terraces in its bare marble version, Venus accommodates a fiberglass shell covered in leather to add comfort in its indoor version.Limited series of 8 copies, available in different varieties of marble.
