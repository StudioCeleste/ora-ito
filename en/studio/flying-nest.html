“FLYING NEST” the new concept of nomadic and ephemeral accommodation from AccorHotels designed by Ora ïto is established in Avoriaz 1800

accommodation at the foot of the slopes, a unique experience in the heart of nature and an exceptional view, we rush to discover the new ephemeral flying nest concept designed by ora ïto.An ambitious idea and an inspired designer, this is the Flying Nest cocktail: offering mobile and unusual accommodation from fully furnished containers with all the comfort of a cozy room, which does not alter the natural landscape and has a low environmental impact. It is Ora-Ïto, one of the most renowned designers of his generation, who signed the eco-design of these rooms using raw and natural materials.unusual accommodation but not only that…!If the Flying Nest concept is totally unusual in terms of its conception, design and architecture, it also offers an experience between comfort, well-being and proximity to nature. You will have a bright living space, a bedroom with a cozy bed, a modern bathroom and a private terrace. In addition to access to a common living space with a view of the mountain to enjoy a gourmet breakfast, a ski room will keep your equipment warm until the next descents. All that remains is to put on your skis in the morning and hit the slopes. In short, premium comfort for a unique and unforgettable experience right in the middle of the Avoriaz ski area.Avoriaz 1800, a resort that moves.An architecture, a concept, preserved nature, in the heart of one of the largest ski areas in the world. Avoriaz, the eternal, the timeless, the avant-garde, the first entirely pedestrian high-altitude resort where Taxis are horses and sleighs, labeled a great achievement of 20th century heritageècentury, continues to reinvent itself. Ideally located in the center of the “Portes du Soleil”, one of the largest ski areas in the world, this resort gives you access to the most beautiful panoramas and 600 km of slopes. The massif benefits from guaranteed snow from December to April, with an average of 12 meters of cumulative snowfall per winter. Here, you ski, surf with family or friends in magnificent landscapes lined with fir trees. With an impressive events program, the station organizes major events:Rock On Snow, Party Fun Live, RFM Winter Week, Rock the Piste…there will be something for everyone.Flying Nest in 2018.For a year, the project was tested in the pilot phase, being present in several operations: in June 2017, in partnership with the “Welcome Fans” operation, Flying Nest was installed atClairefontainea few meters from the castle and the players of the French team to accommodate the winners of the operation. Then it's up to24 hours of Le Mansand to theBordeaux Architecture Biennalethat a showroom was installed in order to present and test the concept. After these various operations, the Flying Nest concept entered the BtoB development phase from January 2018. By being present on the24 hours of Le MansandLe Mans Classicjust 6 meters from the track, last June and July, customers were able to live a unique and exceptional experience to thrill to the rhythm of these legendary endurance races. At the beginning of July, Flying Nest positioned itself at the heart of one of the most prestigious cultural events,The International Photography Meetings in Arles. A temporary beach, a bar, DJ sets, food trucks have enriched the experience... The concept allows you to live your passions at the heart of the events and share unforgettable moments in unique places. This winter, Flying Nest will be located in the heart of the ski area ofAvoriaz.
