Gare d'Austerlitz - Ora ïto x Daniel Buren

CategoryArchitecture
BrandOra ïto Concept
Year 2015

Concept for the redevelopment of Austerlitz station and its large hall by designer Ora ïto and artist Daniel Buren.

In 2030, the station will receive 30 million additional passengers, which requires a profound change. The objective is to improve exchanges between the underground station and the surface station, enable significant commercial development in the area, and provide a shared living environment between travelers, local residents and employees in the area.

Design by Ora ïto withFrancois Leclerq, urban planner architectsPerrot & Richard heritage architects
