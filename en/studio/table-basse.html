Arborescence coffee table

Category Furniture
Brand Christofle
Collection Christofle
Year 2011

For this new collection, Ora-Ito extends its reflection on organic and plant forms. By playing on curves and counter-curves. Elegance, purity, abstraction, symmetry and asymmetry… such is the vocabulary of this new variation of a strong and figurative line – the first piece of which was launched last year. Arborescence is enriched with new creations while respecting the designer's initial approach. Faithful to its philosophy of “simplexity”, or the art of giving an object with complex functions an apparent simplicity, Ora-Ito relied on the know-how and DNA of Christofle to create a natural sequel to the first collection.

For this season, he imagined a 21-light centerpiece with multiple silver metal branches poetically treated like the initial candelabra. Two other creations complete this series: a floor lamp with geometric lines equipped with opal polyphane lampshades, as well as a pedestal table with a graphic tree base topped with a smoked glass top. These creations are available in limited editions of 20 copies each. Christofle and Ora-Ito continue their creative approach in the world of the house.
