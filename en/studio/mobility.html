Giotto

Category Technology
Brand Ora ïto

Year 2014

Red Dot Design Award Winner 2014

View the collection The Gïotto helmet is characterized by its deliberately structured silhouette. On its scale, it echoes the creations of the Ora ïto studio. A flagship piece of the collection, Gïotto mixes machined metal parts with a fabric covering. A tactile encounter that marks the influence of furniture design. Little extra: a docking jack allows you to share your music with your friends.
  • Superior sound quality
  • Adjustable/adjustable headband
  • Foldable arch for easy transport
  • Seamless fabric flat cable
  • Welcome Jack to share his music
  • Ergonomic ear cushions for better listening comfort
  • Built-in microphone for calls
  • Remote control for line pickup and volume adjustment
Speaker diameter: 40mm Impedance: 32 Ohm Power: 20mw-30mw Sensitivity: 120dB +/- 3dB Frequency response: 20Hz-20KHz
