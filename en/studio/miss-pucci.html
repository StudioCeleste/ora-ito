Miss Pucci

CategoryCosmetics
BrandPucci
Year 2010

Starting from the emblematic print, Ora ïto chose the expression of the “bubble” pattern, a small oval bubble which is repeated over and over on fabrics, notably Vivara. From this absolutely Pucci iconic 2D motif, he created a 3D volume to embody the fundamental codes of the house in a precious bottle. Miss Pucci is an evolving, ergonomic and fluid setting whose central point is a window open to the world... A kaleidoscopic stained glass window constantly playing with light.

The bottle is adorned with the shine of silver details which give it the appearance of a jewel. The choice of metal, engraving and monogram inscribe the bottle with a desire for precious sophistication and quality.
