Ora-Gami coffee table

Category Furniture
Brand Steiner
Collection Steiner
Year 2010

“Double skin” coffee table made up of two materially distinct sheets allowing different dimensions to be offered, by sliding the slats. One in champagne satin aluminum, or in a matte black version, the other in black tempered glass gives a play of opacity and transparency to this strategically central piece.

Ora-Gami combines clean lines and technicality for an astonishing result. The armchair for example, designed in polyurethane, comes from a single sheet folded and worked by bending. With a graphic design, a magical effect: coupled with its footrest, the whole thing seems to be suspended on invisible threads. The coffee table, for its part, with its “double skin” effect, allows you to play with its dimensions by sliding the slats on its surface. Modularity that is also found in low furniture, which offers multiple types of storage.
