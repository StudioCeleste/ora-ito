Armchair with footrest Ora-Gami

CategoryFurniture
BrandSteiner
CollectionSteiner
Year 2009

The Ora-Gami polyurethane armchair, a true icon of the collection, is characterized by the purity of its line and its graphic volume inspired by the original shape of the armchair. As if suspended in the air, it is the result of true technical prowess.

Constructed from a single sheet folded and worked by bending, the armchair and its footrest, already cult with a perfectly geometric silhouette, seem to levitate thanks to a cantilevered foot stiffened by a visible frame. This timeless ergonomics plays on the mixture of rigidity and flexibility and characterizes the Ora ïto gesture.
