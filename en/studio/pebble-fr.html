Pebble

CategoryBathroom
BrandRonbow
Year 2015

Ronbow Signature Series brings together artists and designers whose creativity and passion inspire the most diverse and luxurious bathroom collection globally.

The PEBBLE collection is defined by what Ora Ito calls “Simplexity”,  the blend between simplicity of form and complexity of function. The collection embodies a philosophical concept and transforms it into a practical application; making what is too complicated simpler. Complex technology, innovative processes, and new materials are combined in a balanced package for the consumer.
