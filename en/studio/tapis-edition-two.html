Tai Ping Edition Two

CategoryFurniture
BrandTai Ping
Year 2016

Colors in abundance, urban lines and geometric mazes. Neon lights and architectural touches. 3D stories and puzzles. Or even superimposed landscapes and images like photographs seen from the sky. Here are some of the stones of youth thrown on the carpet. With Edition Two, Tai Ping invites six designers from different countries to look at weft and pattern. For Tai Ping Edition Two, everyone, budding or already established creator, has woven their personal geography.

The unique “French touch” of the French designer, Ora Ïto, iconic figure of design in the digital age. Among its three rugs, one recalls the nomadic spirit of its accessories, the other two are so many patchwork elements which come together and construct a honeycomb geography, symbolizing the connections of our world of networks with the great tradition of excellence of the Tai Ping factory.
