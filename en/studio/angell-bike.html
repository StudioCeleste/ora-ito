Angell Bike

Category Transportation
Brand Angell
Year 2020
Ora Ito teamed up with entrepreneur Marc Simoncini to create the Angell bike. With a sleek rounded frame and weighing just 13.9kg, the e-bike offers a smart and effortless way to get around town. The idea for the bike came to Marc Simoncini in the spring of 2017 when he asked "how can we create the best electric bike in the world?" After enlisting the help of Ora Ito, the pair created a magnificent streamlined design in aluminum and carbon. The design features a touchscreen on the handlebars, allowing users to be informed of weather forecasts, air quality information, battery levels, speed and calories burned. The Angell bike also has a removable battery weighing less than 2 kg and hyperbolic indicator lights. In addition to being one of the lightest electric bikes in the world, Angell also offers users three different riding modes: free ride, navigation and sport, with a maximum speed of 25 km/h and a range of 70 km. For peace of mind for the future owner, the battery has an automatic locking system protecting it from theft and recharges in less than two hours.
