Arrow

CategoryPrototype
BrandWhiteMoon - Gallery
CollectionLimited edition
Year 2012

Ora ïto acquired its notoriety very young with the most common and most innovative objects. His conception of design can be summed up in a neologism: “simplexity”, a fusion of simple & complexity. It’s about integrating as much complexity as possible into a refined envelope. The surface is soft, polished, promoting sensual and intuitive contact with its guest occupant. To be discovered in preview at Art Paris from March 29 to April 1, 2012.
