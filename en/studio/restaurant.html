MARXITO Restaurant

CategoryArchitecture
BrandMarxito
Year 2018

Cooked by Thierry Marx Designed by Ora Ito

The combined talent of two big names serving beautiful, good and ethical street food.It is an original and committed project that Marxito, a restaurant ofstreet fooddesigned and developed by starred chef Thierry Marx and the little prince of design Ora Ito. The alliance of a strong requirement in the quality of everyday food and a permanent concern for a design combining, with simplicity, beauty and functionality. The combination of two talents and a manifesto: eating fast and eating healthy.  An original design serving user-friendlinessLover of Japanese minimalist aesthetics, original and avant-garde designer (he is at the origin of projects as diverse as the Mamo at the Cité Radieuse in Marseille or the offices of the LVMH media division) Ora Ito signs the interior design of Marxito. And because when it comes to gastronomy everything happens around the table, he designed an endless table, all curvature, descending from the ceiling before transforming into a surface around which the guests sit. A living space where you can eat all day, take a break or check your emails between two appointments, Marxito offers a soft and warm atmosphere, reinforced by light colors and natural materials.
