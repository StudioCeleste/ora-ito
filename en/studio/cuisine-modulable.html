Foodshelf modular kitchen

CategoryKitchen
BrandScavolini
Year 2012

For Scavolini, European leader in kitchens, Ora ïto has designed a new concept of space open to the living room. Like a functional Lego set, inspired by Mondrian, this line offers a unique modular design to harmonize, in resonance, these two living spaces face to face.

From the same matrix, light oak slats and matt or lacquered modules, vary the combinations, sizes and colors to arrange bookcases, storage, desks, kitchen units, etc. at will.

Style fades to make way for personalization. Tangy touches, refined elegance, original compositions: everything is possible. Easy as child's play.
