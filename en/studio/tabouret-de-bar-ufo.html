UFO bar stool

Category Furniture
Brand Vondom
Collection Vondom
Year 2015
The collection is the result of “genetic mutation experiments” between the iconic languages ​​of pagodas and UFOs. UFO is characterized by the opposition of negative and positive forms. This creative approach resulted in a monolithic, fluid and sculptural line inspired by Marta Pan that encourages comfort and relaxation. The chair, table and sofa allow you to create different compositions, easily adaptable to outdoor spaces thanks to their elegant and resistant materials. The colors, range of finishes and interior lighting expand the possibilities of creating different environments.
