Photo frame

Category Accessories
Brand Christofle
Collection Christofle
Year 2011

With this collection with a divinatory name, Ora-ïto subtly reconnects with antiquity, and does so in the greatest simplicity.

For this second collection, Ora-ïto has imagined creations with mineral influences. The letter opener has something primitive and recalls a flint eroded by time. The paperweight and the votive have the softness of pebbles polished by the foam of the waves. Elegant, precious and discreet on a desk or on the shelves of a library, the gelular frame in silver metal opts for the softness of memories with its simple lines with rounded corners.

Same sense of simplicity with the square silver metal pocket and the white Corian centerpiece, worked like a gentle tectonic in the confrontation of these two materials rarely combined.
