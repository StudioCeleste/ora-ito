Block 5 knives

Category Tableware
Brand Guzzini
Collection Guzzini
Year 2013

The five-knife block with base consists of: meat knife, bread knife, kitchen knife, utility knife and carving knife.

This Guzzini novelty is strongly distinguished by its base made of quality acrylic material with the white front, a solid color and the transparent or transparent red rear to allow the blades of the knives to be seen and facilitate the choice depending on the food to be cut.

In addition, it is removable and therefore easy to clean, thus guaranteeing excellent hygiene. The knives, whose surface finish on the blades is very careful, have a “soft touch” handle and a finger protection function for the safety of the whole family.
