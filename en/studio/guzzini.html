My Kitchen Collection

Category Tableware
Brand Guzzini
Collection Guzzini
Year 2013

Ora Ito has designed for Guzzini a series of items designed for food preparation which includes: a knife block, a kitchen utensil kit with a base, an electronic scale. In addition, for the kitchen or elsewhere, Ora Ito offers us a large wall clock that will become the protagonist of the space it will inhabit.

“The simpler the object is to use, the more intuitive, easy and natural the encounter with the consumer becomes”

The five-knife block with base consists of: meat knife, bread knife, kitchen knife, utility knife and carving knife. Its dimensions are compact: the space occupied on the kitchen worktop is reduced and corresponds to current home requirements. This Guzzini novelty is strongly distinguished by its base made of quality acrylic material with the white front, a solid color and the transparent or transparent red rear to allow the blades of the knives to be seen and facilitate the choice depending on the food to be cut. In addition, it is removable and therefore easy to clean, thus guaranteeing excellent hygiene. The knives, whose surface finish on the blades is very careful, have a “soft touch” handle and a finger protection function for the safety of the whole family. Made in two different color options, gray with transparent base and red, the five knife block with base is made in Italy and dishwasher safe.

The kitchen utensil set with base consists of five utensils: large spoon, spaghetti spoon, slotted spoon, frying spatula and ladle, everything used for preparing and serving food. The utensils are suitable for all types of pans, both in steel and with an internal non-stick coating thanks to the plastic material they are made from, which is scratch-resistant and resistant to high temperatures. The wide base of the structure guarantees very good stability and an innovative system for hanging utensils on the base gives the product an original and truly innovative shape. In addition, the particular shape of the handle, also designed to hang utensils on the base, is a guarantee of hygiene because it prevents the utensil from touching the support surface. Ergonomic, with a “soft touch” handle, functional, dishwasher safe, these utensils will be available in the gray and red version.

The Slim electronic kitchen scale looks like the latest tablets: sober, clean, smooth on the surface, it invites tactile touch. It is available in different colors: wisteria, gray, orange and red. Its maximum capacity is 5 kg and has a double g/oz measurement, tare function, auto-shutoff and four anti-slip feet which guarantee its stability. The electronic kitchen scale from Ora-Ito for Guzzini offers a large and practical white LED display that makes reading easy.

The block of 5 knives with base, the utensil kit with base and the electronic kitchen scale are all accessories to place, to display on the worktop and to use daily: the perfect synthesis between aesthetics and functionality, logic and creativity enhanced by the colors, by the transparencies and by the thickness of the acrylic material.

The latest proposal signed by the French designer for the start of 2013 is the Times Square wall clock.

Its simple and elegant style allows it to match all furniture and in different rooms: from the kitchen to the living room to the office. Its raised graphics and dimensions make it easy to read, its two-tone effects, white frame and red or gray dial, provide a unique and precious character. Its movement is silent and continuous. These are all good reasons to buy one or to give it as a gift, right?
