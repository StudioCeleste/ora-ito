Unexpected

CategoryAccessories
BrandMandarina duck
CollectionMandarina Duck
Year 2012

Attentive, contemporary, functional and original, UNEXPECTED is inspired by the “1:3” ratio, in both proportions and content. UNEXPECTED is a “three in one”. One bag inside the other: in the inner bag, removable and adjustable dividers optimize spaces for the working woman. The interior is extracted, the exterior takes on flexibility without losing functionality, ideal for the city or a weekend getaway.

Without the exterior, the interior bag matches for a night out with friends! The material? Washable coated fabric for the base of the outer bag, treated wrinkle-resistant polyester for the upper part of the outer bag. The inner bag is made of polyester. Wrinkles on the bag? Just rub the fabric, the wrinkles disappear. Unparalleled mischief for the UNEXPECTED bag!
