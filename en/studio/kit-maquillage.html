Makeup Kit

Category Cosmetic
Brand Farrugia
Year 2009

Fred Farrugia launches his first cosmetics brand. An ambition guided by the sole passion of offering women intelligent materials, easy to apply, even without a mirror, totally portable and customizable makeup. From dream to reality, there was only one step left, packaging. Fred wanted it to be resolutely contemporary, practical, beautiful and simple at the same time. His meeting with Ora-Ïto will be decisive, almost magical. From the same generation, these two men understand each other immediately. A discussion will be enough to create in the mind of the designer “the palette” with products in the form of “modules” which are compiled and clipped to each other in a single gesture to reinvent their makeup every day according to their desires. The “Fred Farrugia” makeup kit offers a complete and cosmopolitan range of 104 references for the complexion, eyes and lips.
