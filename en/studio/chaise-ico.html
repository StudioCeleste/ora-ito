Ico chair

Category Furniture
Brand Cassina
Year 2016

With Ico, Ora ïto is part of the legendary heritage of Cassina alongside Ico Parisi, Giovanni Ponti, Gaetano Pesce, Le Corbusier, Charlotte Perriand, Philippe Starck And Patricia Urquiola. A unique know-how, at the exact point of balance between craftsmanship and industry, that the Italian manufacturer makes available to designers and architects capable of redrawing the boundaries of style and experimentation. Thanks to a permanent exchange with the Cassina workshop for more than two years, Ora ïto traces a new archetype of comfort with distinguished elegance.

Naturally grained, the wood is now innervated by the dynamic fluidity of the French designer. A nervous feature, recognizable at first glance, which animates the traditional wooden seat with a new tension to revive the luxury of contemporary living rooms and restaurants. A nod to Ico Parisi and its 814 chair (a Cassina classic designed in 1950), Ico is an enveloping armrest chair in ash covered in fabric or leather, whose stitching perfectly highlights the organic structure like a delicate articulation.

The Ico chair is representative of the values ​​that Cassina still supports today: a combination of carpentry work and technological innovation. This is the concept of the MedaMade project.

Developed over more than two years with French designer Ora Ito, this project managed to completely define the balance between craftsmanship and industry, between style and experimentation.

Ico clearly refers to the history of Cassina, its ‘techno-organic’ nature inspired by industrial/technical elements, was developed in close collaboration with the Cassina Research and Development Center team. The brand has studied in detail the engineering and constructive definition of each component of this apparently simple design, using the most advanced technologies.

The support, made entirely of solid wood, represents the ability to industrialize sinuous and complex shapes through a perfect dialogue between craftsmanship and technology. Indeed, this element is characterized by precious junctions of traditional cabinetmaking, and also by complex creations of 5-axis numerical control milling machines, of the very latest generation. And the comfort of the chair has not been sacrificed for the benefit of technology. Ico's bentwood structure is combined with detailed upholstery work: soft inserts covered in fabric or leather are enriched with stitching details, from the end of the armrest to the backrest, a choice not only functional but also aesthetic.

Ico is available in 4 open-pore matte finishes (natural ash or black, walnut or red stained ash), making this chair versatile and suitable for different architectural contexts. Thanks to its lowered armrests, Ico finds its place around a lunch table. Solid ash support. Ash seat/back. Foam padding. Fabric or leather covering. Matte finishes, semi-open pore, natural ash or black stained ash, stained ash, walnut or red stained ash.
