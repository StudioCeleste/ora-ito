NANO Pop-Up Restaurant

CategoryArchitecture
BrandOra ïto Concept
Year 2013

A temporary restaurant located in the Étienne Marcel district, dear to Ora ïto, the Nano is the creator's very first restaurant, halfway between an art gallery and a contemporary canteen.

Being able to choose as you wish between 25 salty and sweet units: the Nano concept was born. A set of colored units to choose from, according to your appetite and desires, like pixels of flavors to assemble at your convenience. No more “starter, main course and dessert” dictate, Nano is shaking up catering by removing preconceived ideas and putting the desires and tastes of the consumer at the heart. The contemporary canteen makes the restaurant an experience awakening all the senses, combining culinary aesthetics and an arty atmosphere.

“The transparency of the container matches its desire not to camouflage or distort the product. »

The gourmet variation was entrusted to Olivier Chaput, itinerant chef and disciple of Escoffier, young chef from France in 2002. A radical chef, he immediately adopted the concept of the Nano: the transparency of the container matches his desire not to camouflage or distort the product.

The gourmet offering changes with the seasons and two “of the day” recipes are offered according to inspiration. Twisting classics and unexpected creations, the chef works with ultra-fresh products, drawing inspiration from his travels. Roquefort-pear coquillettes, regressive and bold; raw zucchini tagliatelle and smoked salmon, a tangy and ultra fresh mixture; ceviche of sea bream, pomegranate and iceberg, exotic and invigorating; Viennese egg tartare, creamy and spicy... so many choices that demonstrate the chef's creativity and are inspired by 'simplexity' - a concept dear to Ora ïto.

In a sensory decor, combining the warmth of wood and the felted texture of Kvadrat fabric, a unique creation is displayed on the wall: inspired by kinetic art, a true homage to Vasarely, a wave of pixels is made entirely of oak wood by Ora ïto, the signature of this first restaurant. The felt alcove, cozy and warm, envelops the customer as if in a refined cocoon. The room combines formal purity and functional rationalism. Contemporary and urban, the Nano takes each customer on a journey to another taste dimension.
