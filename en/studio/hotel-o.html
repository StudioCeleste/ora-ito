Hotel Odyssey

CategoryArchitecture
BrandElegancia Hotels
Year 2012

The deliberate symbiosis between nature and urban planning.

In the heart of the famous Halles district, near the Louvre and the Seine, discover the Odyssey Hotel, designed by Ora ïto and his team. The Odyssey Hotel is a haven of peace where well-being and comfort reign. In Ora ïto's original style, the hotel is a harmonious blend of clean lines and soft, relaxing curves.

Its 29 rooms welcome you in a space protected from the outside and the stress of the city. The Odyssey will be a fantastic discovery for you.

The Odyssey Hotel aims to provide an experience of joy and relaxation after an eventful day in Paris.
