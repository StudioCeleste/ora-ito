Perfect complementarity

Real and visual lightness combine wonderfully thanks to technological prowess: Obvious, different and pure glasses. Frames that hide their complexity under an apparent simplicity going to the essential: the perfect illustration of the simplicity invented by Ora ïto and brought to music with Lightec technology. The ultra-light Lunamide over-injected around a thin stainless steel blade and the patented flexible hinge without screws or springs combine to give an obvious product, as if suspended from the line of the artist's pencil. The double continuous line creates a fluid shape that reinvents luxury standards: real lightness and visual lightness combine wonderfully. Obvious, different and pure frames.

Optical frames

An ultra-comfortable aerial creation equipped with a patented flexible hinge without screws or springs: 11 grams of lightness and flexibility combining injected LUNAMIDE and stainless steel. Four versions of the iconic Ora ïto frame, combining design and lightness. Fine and structured models for absolute comfort.

Lightec and Ora ïto

Lightec is a concentrate of lightness and technology for immediate comfort: the setting is ideal for designer Ora ïto to infuse his contemporary vision of simplexity in eyewear, or the art of giving complex objects an apparent simplicity.

Morel

The brand uses lightweight materials and a patented flexible hinge system without spring and without screws, to provide immediate and lasting comfort. For this creation, an ultra-light complex polyamide, LUNAMIDE, was developed to inject futuristic beveled shapes, around an ultra-thin stainless steel core.
