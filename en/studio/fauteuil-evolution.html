Evolution armchair

CategoryFurniture
BrandZanotta
CollectionZanotta
Year 2007

A more than complete armchair, as if we had taken all the options to make it not only comfortable, but also reassuring and statutory. Its ergonomics ideally fits the body. The headrest part can be enriched with an integrated audio system and thus become an additional source of relaxation and entertainment. The interior is leather-wrapped gel, and the exterior is fiberglass. The base is made of polished steel giving the effect of a mirror to better fade.
