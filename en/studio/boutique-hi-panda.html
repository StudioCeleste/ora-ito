Hi Panda Shop

CategoryArchitecture
BrandHi Panda
Year 2011

Building on its immense success in Asia with nearly 50 stores in China and Japan, our little Panda has decided to come and set up in Europe and more precisely in Paris. The first store will therefore open its doors in one of the capital's essential districts, at 15 place du Marché Saint Honoré in the 1st arrondissement.

Ora ïto imagined an automorphic architecture to embody the Hi Panda boutique and the ultra-contemporary spirit of the phenomenon brand. By appropriating the extraordinary versatility of Krion, the new generation resin from the Porcelanosa group, he created a radical and refined universe. The Krion can be shaped to meet any design requirements. Ora ïto interprets this modular material to compose a monochromic capsule connected to a symbolic mirrored wall constantly playing between the real and the virtual.

The “pod” is personified by a giant and iconic sculpture deliberately cut in two. The reflection is perfectly symmetrical like each element of the space. He was able to translate the very essence of the identity, with a concern for extreme purity to highlight the Hi Panda graphic universe and the values ​​of the brand expressed in all of its products: we will of course find clothes, toys, paintings, sculptures and limited editions by Jiji, creator of the brand.
