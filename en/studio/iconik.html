Iconic

In 2006, Heineken once again asked Ora-ïto to imagine the evolution of the first Paco aluminum packaging. Ora-ïto then created a new bottle with a long neck silhouette, icon of an entire generation: ICONIK. The aluminum bottle transforms into a slender, unique and emblematic statement seeming to come straight from the future. Heineken reaffirms its pioneering role in the world of contemporary design.

Night Instant

Rare and ephemeral like a moth, the Heineken NIGHT INSTANT bottle is only revealed in the dark. Ora-ïto had the idea of ​​playing on the specificity of UV lighting to design a decor that only reveals its true nature at night. The phosphorescent inks then transform the appearance of the bottle in a disconcerting way: only the pixels light up and the rest of the bottle, plunged into darkness, seems to fade away.

Green Line

This 2009 edition is a nod to the French's favorite beer brand, revealing only fine green curves on the raw material. Minimal graphics to enhance the sculptural dimension and perfect volume of its exclusive creation. This time Ora-ïto imagines a covering, elegant and refined, magnifying the contours of the bottle.

Pure Edition

Ora-ïto imagines for the 2010 series, a refined covering, emphasizing the contours of the “long neck” silhouette. For the first time, the bottle is adorned in white with only minimal graphics, a wave of Heineken pictograms. White reveals the subtlety of lines and the elegance of a material. Far from the brand's usual color codes, Pure surprises with the abstraction of details.
