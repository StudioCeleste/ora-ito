Bump

CategoryFurniture
BrandDunlopillo
CollectionDunlopillo
Year 2012

Bump is reserved for those who want to give personality to their living room. Reserved for modern minds, Bump adapts to all interiors. Straight or corner sofa, left or right, it can do it all. At Dunlopillo, Bump means boldness.

By creating a line of “new generation” seats for Dunlopillo, Ora-Ito wanted to invent a modular and easy-to-use collection: sofa, lounge, pouf, convertible devoid of any complex handling. From small to large interiors, models that suit all lifestyles.
