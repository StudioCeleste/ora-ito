Automatic inheritance

Category Jewelry
Brand Vacheron Constantin
Collection Limited edition
Year 2024
Patrimony automatic: a limited edition born from a long collaboration with Ora ïto. Inspired by a 1957 model, the Patrimony collection launched in 2004 pays homage to the great watchmaking tradition, hiding a complex design behind its sobriety. The celebration of the 20th anniversary of the collection ends with a new automatic reference limited to 100 copies, the result of the collaboration with the designer Ora ïto. A minimalist aesthetic rooted in the 1950s The 1950s were characterized by the creation of classic watch calibers whose finesse matched the beauty of their construction. The ultra-thin 1001 and 1003 hand-wound movements from Vacheron Constantin are one of them. They then instrument the references 6179 and 6187 which occupy a special place in the history of the House. The caliber 1001, barely 2.94mm thick, has a specific construction with five arabesque bridges. Certified Hallmark of Geneva, it enters the chronometric category thanks to its precision regulator. It prefigures the caliber 1003 launched on the occasion of the bicentenary of the House in 1955, then the thinnest movement in the world (1.64mm thick), and today still considered one of the most qualitative hand-wound creations. With the advent of the wristwatch in the first half of the 20th century, the creation of extremely fine models was considered an aesthetic choice and a sign of watchmaking talent. Since the 1950s, flat and ultra-thin watches have become a real issue within the profession. Inspired by these creations, Vacheron Constantin launched the Patrimony collection in 2004: born from classic watchmaking, it is distinguished by a sober design and rare elegance. During the twenty years following the launch, the Patrimony models have never deviated from these principles, marked by a round case and its thin bezel, as well as a refined and domed dial with slender and curved applied indexes, curved baton hands and a beaded minute track. This apparent simplicity of form demands great quality of execution. The almost invisible curvature of the dial requires rigor in the stamping which is calculated to the micron. Achieving the curve given to the hands, indexes and beads of the timer requires the same attention to detail, each machined component having to be reworked by hand with this additional difficulty linked to their tiny size. Aesthetic sobriety – a paradoxically complex exercise in style – responds to fine watchmaking mechanics. The House designs several ultra-thin movements, simple or with complications, such as the caliber 1731 with manual winding and minute repeater and the automatic caliber 1120 with perpetual calendar, respectively 3.90 mm and 4.05 mm thick.  All the models in the collection are clocked by mechanical movements ranging from three-hand displays to noble complications, including variations with special displays such as retrograde date and day. The fruit of a long collaboration

''A good design was not created to be thrown away after a period of use, it is designed around the essentials to make it eternal''. Ora ïto

The aesthetic universes of Patrimony and Ora Ïto are made to come together. The face of the collection since 2019 as part of the “One of Not Many” campaign, the French designer is a long-time admirer of the Patrimony collection, which embodies his concept of “Simplexity” in watchmaking. A neologism combining two a priori contradictory notions, simplicity and complexity, it consists of the art of proposing a simple answer to a complicated problem, of offering a simple appearance to an object which incorporates an invisible complexity. From architecture to furniture, perfumery, telephony and public transport, Ora ïto stands out through constant reinvention and design that focuses on the essential. The new Patrimony automatic is in watchmaking illustration. A signature piece Sober, essential, timeless, the new Patrimony automatic celebrates the apparent simplicity that characterizes the collection. Limited edition of 100 pieces, it has a 40mm yellow gold case which opens onto a tone-on-tone gold dial, decorated with an infinity of concentric circles catching the light. Very visually pure, this circular geometry, which unfolds like a wave, echoes the domed surface of the dial. Passionate about astronomy, Ora ïto explains that “this circular movement of light refers to the Pearls of Baily, a spectacular phenomenon which accompanies an eclipse of the sun by the moon”. The dominant yellow gold is expressed forcefully: on the applied indexes, on the beaded minute track and on the slightly curved hands which follow the curve of the dial. This multitude of details gives a retro touch to the model, like the date which stands out discreetly at 6 o'clock in a tone-on-tone window or the Vacheron Constantin logo placed under the sapphire crystal in a metallic transfer. On the reverse, the sapphire crystal reveals the automatic manufacture caliber 2450 with hours, minutes, seconds and date with haute horology finishes including, in particular, perlage on the plate, Geneva stripes on the bridges and openwork gold oscillating weight on a Maltese cross. The piece extends onto a burgundy calfskin strap punctuated with softened rectangular shapes which reinforce the vintage character of the watch and contrast with the circular geometry of the case and dial.
