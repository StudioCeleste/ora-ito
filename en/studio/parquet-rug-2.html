Parquet Rug

CategoryFurniture
BrandStepevi
Year 2011

Ora-Ïto has imagined a new collection of rugs, produced in collaboration with Stepevi, built on plays of lines and reliefs. Part of a 100% ecological approach, these creations are entirely made from scraps from Stepevi’s exceptional craftsmanship. The rugs are evolving and reinterpret the blades of an offset parquet floor. The extensions can be plugged in endlessly to renew themselves by playing with color gradients.
