Set of 5 utensils with support

Category Tableware
Brand Guzzini
Collection Guzzini
Year 2013

The kitchen utensil kit with base consists of five utensils: large spoon, spaghetti spoon, slotted spoon, frying spatula and ladle, everything used for preparing and serving food.

The utensils are suitable for all types of pans, both in steel and with an internal non-stick coating thanks to the plastic material they are made from, which is scratch-resistant and resistant to high temperatures. The wide base of the structure guarantees very good stability and an innovative system for hanging utensils on the base gives the product an original and truly innovative shape.

In addition, the particular shape of the handle, also designed to hang utensils on the base, is a guarantee of hygiene because it prevents the utensil from touching the support surface. Ergonomic, with “soft touch” handle, functional, dishwasher safe.
