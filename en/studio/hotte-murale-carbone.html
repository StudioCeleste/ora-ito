Carbon Wall Hood

Category Kitchen
Brand Gorenje
Collection Gorenje
Year 2010

The Gorenje Ora-Ïto collection consists of an assortment of fridge freezers, built-in multifunction ovens, glass ceramic hobs, extractor hoods and decorative panels for integrated dishwashers and microwaves. The devices belong to energy class A and have all the functions that today's user could desire. One of their characteristics is the uniform appearance of black glass fronts with brushed aluminum handles; these are available in black or silver. The new collection is a great choice for anyone who appreciates clean lines and multifunctional home appliances.
