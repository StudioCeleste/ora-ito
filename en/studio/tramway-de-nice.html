ora·ïto — projects — Nice tramway

Nice tram

Category Transportation
Brand Alstom
Collection Alstom
Year 2019
Ora ïto designs the new generation of Alstom Citadis X 05 trams on a city scale to design an organic and rational train. The limpid elegance of the hood traces the path of a contemporary ocher jet designed to slip into the historic city. Ocher like the pigment of the facades of the sumptuous Place Massena or the Villa Matisse, emblematic jewels of the 17th century when Nice was still under Sardinian influence, each train is designed as a summary of Nice architecture. Ora ïto draws on its dual Italian culture to update a tramway synthesis of the Mediterranean city. The imagination of traditional lime work is here transposed into a high-tech object with the simplicity dear to the designer, this relentless decoding of the most complex technologies to simplify them and put them at the service of the user. The ocher-pure tramway is enhanced with black bands to punctuate each train with a graphic and signage rhythm. Sense of detail taken to the extreme, the metallic lacquering will allow the city to be reflected in the movement of the tram to immediately resonate with the landscape. A chameleon object that embodies the French designer's transversal vision for Nice, like an ideal and mobile portrait of the city. In order to integrate perfectly into the Nice landscape, the Ocre tramway will benefit from the innovative solution of static recharging from the ground so as not to install too many overhead contact lines. The least energy-consuming mode of public transport, the tram is today popular with cities.

This new generation will contribute even better to environmental protection with 30% less energy consumed, recovery of braking energy and a recovery rate of 98%.

Each 44 meter train will be able to accommodate 300 passengers. The latest addition to the Alstom range, the Citadis Accessibility is enhanced with double doors along the entire length of the train. Passenger safety is ensured by a real-time video protection system, escape hatches in the event of emergency evacuation and very high fire resistance materials. Delivery of the first trainsets is scheduled for summer 2017 for entry into service in 2018 on the West-East line which will be 11.3 kilometers long with 3.2 kilometers in tunnel. The overall market for the 19 trainsets ordered is 91 million euros.
