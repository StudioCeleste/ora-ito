HO Tree Candelabra

Category Furniture
Brand Christofle
Collection Christofle
Year 2009

Nature is for Ora-Ito an inexhaustible source of inspiration. Indoor sculptures, these candelabras demonstrate a free aesthetic interaction between the idea of ​​wild nature – the tree structure of the silver material which takes on a vegetal appearance in the form of ramifications – and the mastery of architectural lines.

The aesthetic of purity, a value shared by Christofle and Ora-Ito, takes on its full extent with these modern and timeless creations. Fluidity of the lines to the point of approaching as closely as possible the mental representation of the candelabra, here we touch on the very idea of ​​the object, its quintessence.

A new step taken in Christofle's anchoring in the world of decoration, Ora-Ito imagines an exceptional piece, a real silver shrub, created by the Haute Orfèvrerie workshop of the Christofle factory. A decorative piece with multiple silver ramifications, it measures more than 1.20m in height. Numbered and signed, this standing candelabra is limited to 20 copies.
