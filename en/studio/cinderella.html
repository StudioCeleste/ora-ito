Cinderella

Category Accessories
Brand Virtual Products
Collection Virtual Products
Year 2001

This first pair of Ora-Ito “shoes” was designed with Roger Vivier (1907 – 1998). The legendary fashion designer of women's feet invented the stiletto heel for Christian Dior before putting his talent at the service of Yves Saint Laurent. The Cinderella 2001 model is the result of his latest collaboration.
