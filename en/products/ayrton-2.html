ora·ïto — projects — Ayrton

Ayrton

Over-ear headphones
Range Audio
Year 2013

Designed like an audio headset pictogram, the Ayrtön headset lends itself to all customizations. Its multiple colors, its interchangeable headband, make it a modular object, adaptable to all attitudes, to all looks.

  • Interchangeable headband
  • Adjustable/adjustable headband
  • Flat cable
  • Swivel pads for better ergonomics
  • Built-in microphone for calls
  • Remote control for line pickup and volume adjustment
Material
Fabric and Rubber
Specifications
Speaker diameter: 40mm
Impedance: 32 Ohm
Power: 20mw
Sensitivity: 113dB+/-3dB
Frequency response: 20Hz-20KHz
In the box
On-ear headphones and its 2 interchangeable headbands
Soft carrying pouch
