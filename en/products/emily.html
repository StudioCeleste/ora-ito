ora·ïto — projects — Emïly

Emily

Wireless speaker
Range Audio
Year 2013

Designed from materials used in interior architecture, the portable Bluetooth speaker integrates perfectly into the spaces that accommodate it. Its formal codes are reminiscent of those of the Giotto helmet. Equally comfortable indoors or outdoors, it is equipped with a handle that serves as both support and transport.

  • Portable Bluetooth stereo speaker
  • Listen to music wirelessly
  • Simplified connection thanks to the NFC function
  • Superior sound quality
  • Compact and powerful
  • Carrying handle and stand
  • Auto sleep
Material
Brushed metal finish
Available in Kvadrat Tempo 670, Kvadrat Tempo 770, Kvadrat Tempo 950, Kvadrat Tempo 170
Specifications
Bluetooth 4.0
Power: 6W RMS x2
Listening time: up to 8 hours
Impedance: 6 Ohm
Frequency response: 70Hz-20kHz
Built-in battery: 2600mAh/7.4V
Input and output jack
Dimensions: 240x151.2x47.2mm
In the box
Portable Bluetooth speaker
Soft carrying case
Jack audio cable
Mains charger
