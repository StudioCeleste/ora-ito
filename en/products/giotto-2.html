ora·ïto — projects — Gïotto

Giotto

Over-ear headphones
Range Audio
Year 2013

The Gïotto helmet is characterized by its deliberately structured silhouette. On its scale, it echoes the creations of the Ora ïto studio. A flagship piece of the collection, Gïotto mixes machined metal parts with a fabric covering. A tactile encounter that highlights the influence of furniture design. Little extra: a docking jack allows you to share your music with your friends.

  • Superior sound quality
  • Adjustable/adjustable headband
  • Foldable arch for easy transport
  • Seamless fabric flat cable
  • Welcome Jack to share his music
  • Ergonomic ear cushions for better listening comfort
  • Built-in microphone for calls
  • Remote control for line pickup and volume adjustment
Material
Brushed metal, fabric or leather finish
Specifications
Speaker diameter: 40mm
Impedance: 32 Ohm
Power: 20mw-30mw
Sensitivity: 120dB +/- 3dB
Frequency response: 20Hz-20KHz
In the box
Over-ear headphones
Soft carrying case
3.5mm audio cable, length 1.2m
