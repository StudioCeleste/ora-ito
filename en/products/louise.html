Louise

Folio Case for Smartphones
Range Protection
Year 2013

The patterns used on the protective cases (Organic, Padding) are inspired by Ora ïto's architecture, its furniture, its hotel room wall coverings, etc. The variety of materials used gives the collection a transgenerational aspect. The particular care given to the finishes places these high-tech products in the range of the best pieces of contemporary design.

  • Organic quilted effect pattern
  • Ultra-thin design and optimal protection
  • Microfiber interior
  • Front speaker cutout which allows you to make calls with the case closed
  • Available for iPhone 5/5S/6
