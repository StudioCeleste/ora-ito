Pïa

In-ear headphones
Range Audio
Year 2013

Two curved headphones with perfect symmetry, two cables merging to form a single line. An ergonomic remote control that twists naturally to position itself in the right direction. Simplicity, efficiency.

  • Flat cable
  • Built-in microphone for calls
  • Remote control for line pickup and volume adjustment
  • Three tip sizes to fit all ears
Material
“Soft touch” rubber finish

Sensitivity: 90dB +/- 5dB
