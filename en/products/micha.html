Micha

Mains charger
Range Charge
Year 2013

Ultra compact and flexible, the chargers adapt to all sockets and all interiors. Their cable is reminiscent of those of designer lamps. The cable connectors have a sliding protection. The flat cables are slipped into a woven textile, without sewing. A first.

  • Mains charger and dual USB pull-out cable
  • Ultra-thin design
  • Flexible tip for easy access to your socket
  • Allows you to charge 2 devices simultaneously
Material
Base: navy blue or gray rubber finish
Seamless fabric flat cable
Specifications
Power: 2.1A
In the box
1 mains charger base
1 dual USB cable with micro USB or iPhone connection
