Wallpaper* Design Awards 2019 — Marxito: Stealth Health award winner

Date February 9, 2019
Rubric Events
Project Studio

Ora ito and Thierry Marx are awarded a "stealth health" design award by Wallpaper magazine for their Marxito restaurant concept.

STEALTH HEALTH Virtuous victuals served on architectural table toppers. THE SAVOURY Chef Thierry Marx and designer Ora-ïto are changing the fast food game with Marxito, a pink-hued street food diner near Paris’ Champs-Élysées. Inspired by Japanese dorayaki pancakes, the Marxito sandwich contains a range of fillings, such as avocado, smoked mozzarella, tofu and pickled radish. All ingredients come from small or local producers.
