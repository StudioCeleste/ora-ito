<p class="lede">The Gïotto helmet is characterized by its deliberately structured silhouette. On its scale, it echoes the creations of the Ora ïto studio. A flagship piece of the collection, Gïotto mixes machined metal parts with a fabric covering. A tactile encounter that highlights the influence of furniture design. Little extra: a docking jack allows you to share your music with your friends.</p>
<div class="desc"><ul>
<li>Superior sound quality</li>

<li>Seamless fabric flat cable</li>
<li>Welcome Jack to share his music</li>
<li>Ergonomic ear cushions for better listening comfort</li>
<li>Built-in microphone for calls</li>
