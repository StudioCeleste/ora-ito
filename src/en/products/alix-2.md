<p class="lede">A tribute to Le Corbusier's color palette, a nod to MAMO, the art center of the city of Marseille designed by the great architect, the Alix shell goes beyond protection. It revisits the design of the iPhone and softens it thanks to its beveled angles.</p>
<div class="desc"><ul>
<li>Organic pattern matte/shiny effect</li>
<li>Non-slip, non-deformable, it offers ideal protection</li>
<li>Beveled corners that absorb shock</li>
<li>Access to all the features of your mobile</li>
<li>Available for iPhone 5/5S</li>
</ul></div>
