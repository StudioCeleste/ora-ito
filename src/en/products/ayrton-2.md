<p class="lede">Designed like an audio headset pictogram, the Ayrtön headset lends itself to all customizations. Its multiple colors, its interchangeable headband, make it a modular object, adaptable to all attitudes, to all looks.</p>
<div class="desc"><ul>
<li>Interchangeable headband</li>
<li>Adjustable/adjustable headband</li>
<li>Flat cable</li>
<li>Swivel pads for better ergonomics</li>
<li>Built-in microphone for calls</li>
<li>Remote control for line pickup and volume adjustment</li>
</ul></div>
