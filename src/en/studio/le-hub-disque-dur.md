<p style="text-align: justify;">A unique design. Expertly combining form and function, the LaCie Huby stands out as much for its unique design as for its comprehensive connectivity features. The Huby was created exclusively for LaCie by Ora-ïto, the internationally renowned creator of The Brick and other remarkable products. Made of robust glossy white polycarbonate, it does not go unnoticed with its round shape like a light bulb from which flexible cables emerge. Each port is connected to a colored LED, which lights up in the case when a device is plugged in.</p>
