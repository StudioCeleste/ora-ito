Best of the Best Category</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The world leader in the publishing and selective distribution of furniture presents, exclusively, a complete collection, signed Ora ïto: high table, coffee table, sofa, sideboard and pedestal table.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Emblematic element of this line: the table. Cantilevered on three twisting uprights, this piece combines design and technical prowess to defy the laws of gravity. Connected to the ground by a triple twisted arm, its tempered glass top seems ready to spin on itself, like a top. A whirlwind with an innovative breath.</p>
