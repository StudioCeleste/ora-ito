<p style="text-align: justify;">This “One Line Vice” version offers a vice anchoring system allowing more space on the work surface and turning the body of the lamp on the horizontal plane to better manage the orientation of the light flux. The diffuser has a red screen and stands out slightly in relation to the profile of the arm, the presence of which it highlights.</p>
