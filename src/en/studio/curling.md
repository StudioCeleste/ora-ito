<p style="text-align: justify;">The round sofa. Small sofa for two, very round, very soft, the Curling finds its ideal place in all young and modern interiors. By creating a line of “new generation” seats for Dunlopillo, Ora-Ito wanted to invent a modular and easy-to-use collection: sofa, lounge, pouf, convertible devoid of any complex handling. From small to large interiors, models that suit all lifestyles.</p>
