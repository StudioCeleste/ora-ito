Best Package Design</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">In 2002, Heineken called on the very young designer Ora-Ïto to design a luxurious setting for the nightlife world. With spectacular support from the French public, the bottle was then launched successively in 35 countries with the same success! Establishing a new deal on the market, this sophisticated-looking bottle soon became a reference in the nightlife world but also in the media, making it one of the most publicized beer bottles in history. It even reached legendary status by becoming the acquisition of the National Contemporary Art Fund (FNAC) and is still exhibited today as a work of art in museums and art galleries around the world.</p>
