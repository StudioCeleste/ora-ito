<p style="text-align: justify;">The largest shopping centers equipped with the most beautiful press kiosks with Ora ïto Unibail-Rodamco and Mediakiosk, a subsidiary of Presstalis (formerly NMPP) have launched an original initiative by bringing together three major protagonists of contemporary design (Ora ïto, Patrick Jouin and Matthieu Paillard) to design a completely new press kiosk model, which is intended to be installed in the largest shopping centers of the Unibail-Rodamco group. The latter is notably the owner and manager of Quatre Temps, Forum des Halles, Vélizy 2, Parly 2, Carré Sénart and Lyon Part-Dieu.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Ora ïto’s project was chosen for its innovative creative gesture, which revisits this classic of the French urban landscape; it creates an effect of surprise which fully participates in the experience of a shopping trip.</p>
<p class="feat-p">“An instantly identifiable design, wide open, soft and clear. A true “object”, we have favored simplified lines, a softened silhouette”</p>
The Jury was composed of the following personalities:
• Jean-Paul Abonnenc, Managing Director of Mediakiosk
• Élisabeth Couturier, Journalist specializing in Design
• Franz-Olivier Giesbert, Publication Director of Le Point
• Henri Griffon, President of the National Union of French Furniture Industries
• Rémy Pflimlin, Managing Director of Presstalis
• Guillaume Poitrinal, Chairman of the Unibail-Rodamco Management Board
• Jean-Marie Tritant, General Manager Shopping Centers France Unibail-Rodamco
<p style="text-align: justify;">This initiative is in response to the closure of many specialized press sales outlets for several years in city centers, a difficult context in which publishers, via messaging, are working to strengthen the capillarity of the sales network, in the most direct contact with buyers.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The Unibail-Rodamco Group is carrying out a metamorphosis of its shopping centers, which aim to become, beyond a shopping place popular with the French, a place of life and experiences. Already home to some of the largest cinemas, presenting cultural distribution brands known to all, the large shopping centers will now open up much more widely to the press, which can be purchased and read on site, during a moment of relaxation. These places can offer press publishers unparalleled potential for contact with their readers. In total, the Group's shopping centers welcome around 750 million visits per year, with three quarters of the centers attracting more than 7 million visitors each year. To do this, we must completely renew the presentation of the press offering and win over new readers by rethinking the form, the spirit, and even the very functionalities of the press kiosk. With, for example, the integration of new technologies allowing the implementation of new interactive services.</p>
