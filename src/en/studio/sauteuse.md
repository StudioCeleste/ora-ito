<p style="text-align: justify;">In 2011, Aubecq invited ORA-ÏTO and asked it to design a series of high-end utensils combining two “exclusive” AUBECQ technologies: CNC (Computer Numerical controlled Cutting) and the technological mastery of 3 materials: stainless steel, aluminum and copper.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The technology was designed to maximize conduction power. This ultra-precise cutting digitally controlled by computer reveals the copper and allows your utensil to heat up in record time. By optimizing the heating of your utensils, you reduce your energy consumption while preserving the nutritional and taste qualities of the cooked products.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Over the last 5 years, Aubecq engineers have studied and explored new avenues to find solutions to improve heat absorption. They have succeeded, thanks to the synergy of stainless steel, aluminum and copper, and their position in 5 layers, in reinforcing the conductive efficiency of the 3 materials used. On this range everything has been calculated and recalculated to offer you products combining technology, efficiency, energy saving, design and the pleasure of cooking.</p>
