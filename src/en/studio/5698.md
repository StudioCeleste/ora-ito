<p style="text-align: justify;">The five-knife block with base consists of: meat knife, bread knife, kitchen knife, utility knife and carving knife.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">This Guzzini novelty is strongly distinguished by its base made of quality acrylic material with the white front, a solid color and the transparent or transparent red rear to allow the blades of the knives to be seen and facilitate the choice depending on the food to be cut.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">In addition, it is removable and therefore easy to clean, thus guaranteeing excellent hygiene. The knives, whose surface finish on the blades is very careful, have a “soft touch” handle and a finger protection function for the safety of the whole family.</p>
