<p style="text-align: justify;">Drawing its inspiration from “Simplexity” or the art of giving an object with complex functions an apparent simplicity, Ora-ïto joins forces with HI-MACS and creates a monumental work of art based on the notion of functional art. The quasi-architectural structure is the result of a true technological feat. Thanks to its imperceptible joints, its thermoformed lines, its ergonomics, the aesthetics of the purity and the play of curves and counter-curves, the sculpture reveals a seat which follows the fluid lines of its design. Art becomes functional and useful. Ora-ïto plays with the very quintessence of HI-MACS ® and the key values ​​of its expertise: know-how, quality and innovation. Unique work presented in preview at the 100% Design show in London on September 23, 2010.</p>
