<p style="text-align: justify;">Nature is for Ora-Ito an inexhaustible source of inspiration. Symmetry, asymmetry, organ plays, the torch and the candelabra play with their beautiful proportions and make you want to multiply and assemble them endlessly.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Indoor sculptures, these candelabras demonstrate a free aesthetic interaction between the idea of ​​wild nature – the tree structure of the silver material which takes on a vegetal appearance in the form of ramifications – and the mastery of architectural lines.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The aesthetic of purity, a value shared by Christofle and Ora-Ito, takes on its full extent with these modern and timeless creations. Fluidity of the lines to the point of approaching as closely as possible the mental representation of the candelabra, here we touch on the very idea of ​​the object, its quintessence.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The collection reveals a fun and functional “twist”: an articulated version of the 4-light candelabra allows free arrangement of the silver branches. The base of the candelabra is fixed on a pivot, which allows the object from a functional point of view to offer numerous possibilities for lighting beams, and from an aesthetic point of view, to renew the appearance of the candelabra with each movement. From modularity comes new beauty.</p>
