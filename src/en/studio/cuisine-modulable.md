<p style="text-align: justify;">For Scavolini, European leader in kitchens, Ora ïto has designed a new concept of space open to the living room. Like a functional Lego set, inspired by Mondrian, this line offers a unique modular design to harmonize, in resonance, these two living spaces face to face.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">From the same matrix, light oak slats and matt or lacquered modules, vary the combinations, sizes and colors to arrange bookcases, storage, desks, kitchen units, etc. at will.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Style fades to make way for personalization. Tangy touches, refined elegance, original compositions: everything is possible. Easy as child's play.</p>
