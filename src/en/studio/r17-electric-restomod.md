<b>Based on the iconic Renault 17 sports coupe, R17 electric restomod x Ora ïto is a sculptural reinterpretation of this model born in 1971.</b>

Unique collaborations that reinterpret iconic models. Since 2021, Renault has presented several unique creative projects that reinterpret the extraordinary heritage of the brand, rich in iconic models that are part of pop culture.  These projects show the brand's ability to merge heritage, design and innovation. It is an extraordinary territory of play and expression for contemporary designers. Renault is now calling on the creative imagination of Ora ïto, one of the most avant-garde designers of his generation, to reinvent the aesthetics of Renault 17, thus offering a bold and modern vision of this historic car.

This year, Renault is announcing a new, unprecedented collaboration between designer Ora ïto and the Renault Design teams to bring to life a unique restomod show car based on the legendary Renault 17 model.
<p class="feat-p" style="font-weight: 400;">« <em>Revisiting an iconic vehicle like the Renault 17, by expressing my vision to make it a sculpture that resembles me, was a particularly exciting challenge shared with Renault.</em> » Ora ïto</p>
<b>Arnaud Belloni, Chief Marketing Officer Renault Group and Brand:</b>

« <em>R17 restomod, reinterpreted by Ora ïto, perfectly embodies our vision of uniting heritage and innovation. A true icon of the 70s, R17 has established itself as a figure in pop culture through its avant-garde design and bold character. This project celebrates Renault's iconic design while integrating contemporary elements, offering an experience that transcends generations. At Renault, we are proud to collaborate with daring talents like </em>Ora ïto<em> to reinvent our iconic models while remaining faithful to the spirit of the brand.</em>»

<b>Renault 17: the sports coupe with avant-garde design</b>
<p style="font-weight: 400;">Presented in 1971, Renault 17 was a concentrate of the idealistic explosion of the 1970s. This sporty coupe with clean lines – offered in the form of a four-seater coupe or a convertible – was distinguished by its appearance and performance which perfectly embodied the spirit of the era. Front-wheel drive, longitudinal engine, then the arrival of injection, active and passive safety, Renault 17 relied as much on its technique as on the elegance of its angular lines and its interior designed to attract a demanding clientele. More than 92,000 examples were produced between 1971 and 1979.</p>
<b>Ora ïto: contemporary design signed with simplicity</b>
<p style="font-weight: 400;">R17 electric restomod x Ora ïto is a unique piece which does not foreshadow a future production vehicle.  Designed in the form of a restomod by Ora Ïto, it was developed in collaboration with the Renault Design teams.</p>
<b>Ora ïto:</b>

« <em>With Sandeep (Bhambra) and Gilles (Vidal), we worked together on a retrofuturistic, almost cinematic theme. I wanted to drape Renault 17 d</em><em>’</em><em>a second skin to magnify it and bring it back to our times with my own grammar: fluidity, dynamism, rationality and my signature: simplicity. J</em><em>’</em><em>I wanted to simplify complex elements</em>. »
<p style="font-weight: 400;">An emblematic figure of contemporary design, the Frenchman Ora ïto is a child of the 1970s. Revealed by avant-garde creations and a daring approach, Ora ïto has made a name for himself by hacking the biggest brands with digital products. A fan of simplicity, mixing simplicity and complexity, he is a pop culture phenomenon, which collects the most beautiful collaborations and awards around the world. R17 electric restomod x Ora ïto is its first joint work with Renault.</p>
<p style="font-weight: 400;">With an emphasis on respecting the history and integrity of Renault 17, this show car also incorporates restomod elements, combining the classic charm of the original car with contemporary technologies and materials. This approach creates a synergy between the past and the present, thus offering a modern experience while preserving the very essence of Renault 17.</p>
<b>Retrofuturistic, elegant and stylish version of R17</b>
<p style="font-weight: 400;">A two-door coupe with a hatchback, the Renault 17 featured a sporty design accentuated by its four round headlights, its doors without window frames, its absence of a central pillar, its fully retractable rear side windows and the rear quarter glass decorated with blinds.  Designed on the monocoque structure of the original vehicle, R17 electric restomod x Ora Ïto retains the cabin, doors, windows, glazing and seals as well as the technical support surfaces.</p>
<p style="font-weight: 400;">The car has been widened (+17 cm) for better handling. Its exteriorized fenders and wheels give the car a look that is both spectacular and elegant.</p>
<p style="font-weight: 400;">R17 electric restomod x Ora ïto is resolutely modern as shown by its headlights in four rectangular modules with rounded corners or its rear lights simplified into a single crossing strip. It also includes a 100% electric 270 horsepower engine, located at the rear of the vehicle.</p>
<p style="font-weight: 400;">Its bodywork <em>flush</em>, all in carbon, gives it a contained weight of 1.4 tonnes. It also features an original Galactic Brown color, specifically developed for the show car.</p>
<p style="font-weight: 400;">Inside, like a real passenger vehicle, R17 was equipped with a suitable driving position, comfortable seats, complete equipment and displayed a careful finish. The restomod version maintains this comfortable universe by integrating modifications to the original dashboard and central console. The seats, redesigned around the structure of the original “petal” seats, are adorned with new fabrics inspired by furnishings: a mottled satin in very fine Merino wool for the brown fabric and a light and delicate, high and thick wool Boucle for the beige fabric. Finally, resolutely contemporary, R17 electric restomod x Ora Ïto is equipped with a central screen integrating today's Renault graphic environment and four small geometric screens behind the steering wheel renewing the spirit of period meters.</p>
<b>Ora ïto:</b>
<em>“We have respected the most distinctive elements of Renault 17, starting from its front. Then, I brought fluidity and elegance, but without ever distorting R17. The history of Renault 17 and its sports coupe line are preserved. I wanted to bring my codes by amplifying certain elements to make them more timeless, with more futuristic touches, notably thanks to the rims. The interior also has my signature. We teleported Renault 17 into its future by applying a few subtle developments that changed everything. We had a key phrase: ‘the detail is not a detail’. Everything retains a certain ambivalence, because it is intended to be as playful as it is serious. Renault has a strong identity and I like that.</em> <em>We recognize a Renault and this restomod is no exception to this rule</em>. »

<b>Sandeep Bhambra, Renault Chief Concept Car Designer:</b>
<p style="font-weight: 400;"><em>“The artistic reinterpretation of R17 was a wonderful area of ​​exploration to create something unexpected and memorable. The creative dialogue between Ora ïto and our Design teams restores the silhouette and the very essence of R17 while producing a minimalist, hyper-contemporary object. On board, the major original themes of R17 are reinterpreted with the vision of Ora ïto. The very geometric treatment of shapes takes passengers into the comfortable world of furniture and living. » </em></p>
