<p style="text-align: justify;">To pay homage to the material silver in its most radical sense, Ora ïto has imagined non-ornamental, non-decorative jewelry but universal and timeless. This collection is almost designed as a men's collection for women in order to stay away from fashions that come and go with the vagaries of time and especially in the world of jewelry.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Each of the jewels responds to the same mathematical logic inspired by absolute geometry, formal rationalism and the purity of volumes to offer the eyes only the essential. Ora ïto thus pays a new tribute to the pioneers of Minimal art with timeless pieces which aim to become the new classic of contemporary jewelry.</p>
