<p style="text-align: justify;">Concept for the redevelopment of Austerlitz station and its large hall by designer Ora ïto and artist Daniel Buren.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">In 2030, the station will receive 30 million additional passengers, which requires a profound change. The objective is to improve exchanges between the underground station and the surface station, enable significant commercial development in the area, and provide a shared living environment between travelers, local residents and employees in the area.</p>
