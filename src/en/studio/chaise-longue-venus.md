<strong>Ora Ito</strong> and the Italian marble specialist <strong>Citco</strong> will reveal the chair <strong>Venus</strong> at the Milan Furniture Fair next April. For 20 years, Ora Ito has been building a sensual and rational volume language adapted to each function. With Venus, he creates a sculptural chair like a chosen piece of his own DNA. A Venus with imperious vivacity, whose asymmetry causes spectacular nervous ruptures. The celebration of drawing in space by a visual designer who digitally sculpts marble centimeter by centimeter with incredible dynamic precision.
<p class="feat-p">When the best of Italian craftsmanship meets the vitality of streamline.</p>
The biomorphic, polished curves draw bridges, throw themselves cantilevered into the void, finally call out for a caress. The block of marble is grooved by a hollow joint which traces a rational line on the organic form, underlines its functionality, its ergonomics and domesticates its impetuosity. Naturally intended for gardens and terraces in its bare marble version, Venus accommodates a fiberglass shell covered in leather to add comfort in its indoor version.

<em>Limited series of 8 copies, available in different varieties of marble.</em>
