<p class="feat-p">Red Dot Design Award Winner 2014</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The ORA-ITO range of utensils from AUBECQ was designed and designed by the famous French designer of the same name. His philosophy, called “simplexity”, is the art of giving an object with complex functions a simple appearance. It is through this concept that we discover the 16 cm removable 3PLY saucepan, a utensil with a modern design and practical use for cooking well.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Thanks to the combination of stainless steel and aluminum superimposed in three layers, this saucepan has the advantage of being light and heating up very quickly. It allows food to be cooked quickly and harmoniously, while retaining heat for longer. Its advantages make it economical in terms of energy and preparation time.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The ORA-ITO range is synonymous with maneuverability for a cooking utensil. Its handles are removable and available in two sizes. They are made of nylon reinforced with 25% fiberglass, which makes them flexible and resistant. Cleaning is easy, the different parts are dishwasher safe.</p>
