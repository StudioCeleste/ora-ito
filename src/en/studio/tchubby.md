<p style="text-align: justify;">With an assertive, rounded design, the Tchubby sofas, armchair and pouf give a resolutely contemporary touch to your interior. Tilted comfort as today's generations like it, highlighted by its contrasting piping, Tchubby is made for those who love designer decor.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">By creating a line of “new generation” seats for Dunlopillo, Ora-Ito wanted to invent a modular and easy-to-use collection: sofa, lounge, pouf, convertible devoid of any complex handling. From small to large interiors, models that suit all lifestyles.</p>
