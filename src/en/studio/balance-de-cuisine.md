<p style="text-align: justify;">The Slim electronic kitchen scale looks like the latest tablets: sober, clean, smooth on the surface, it invites tactile touch, is available in different colors.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Its maximum capacity is 5 kg and has a double g/oz measurement, tare function, auto-shutoff and four anti-slip feet which guarantee its stability.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The electronic kitchen scale from Ora-Ito for Guzzini offers a large and practical white LED display that makes reading easy.</p>
