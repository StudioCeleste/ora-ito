<p style="text-align: justify;">Toyota's Parisian meeting turned the idea of ​​the concept store upside down, by aiming to be a theatrical setting that fades away in favor of the cars on display. Toyota wanted to combine it with a place to eat, which Ora ïto thought of as a modular space.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">On the service side, a rounded bar allows you to finish orders initiated in the Hisayuki Takeuchi restaurant, rue André Lefebvre. On the dining room side, it features a combination of tables and chairs which combines the white of Corian, this very resistant synthetic resin which can be sculpted, glued and even thermoformed, and the soft red of the seats. Everything fits together perfectly and can be stacked.</p>
