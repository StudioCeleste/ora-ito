<p style="text-align: justify;">The Laguiole knife is a functional work of art that is part of the historical cultural heritage of the French. It is to pay homage to its historical nobility that Ora-Ïto imagined for Laguiole the new classic of this new millennium: Furtivo. This new refined ergonomics transforms the legendary knife into a contemporary object evoking a new luxury with distinguished elegance. Ora-Ïto has developed through his gesture a completely new physiognomy resulting from the illustrious art of living in the history of Laguiole. The design of the handle is an abstraction of volume punctuating the knife with a through opening in a logic of rational economy of material. Tapered like a boat, the blade has been cut following the same counter-curve as that which made Laguiole so successful, thus fitting the handle when folded.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Fascinated by the strong roots and values ​​from which Laguiole comes, Ora-Ïto has given new letters of nobility to the industrious bee which has always adorned knives. A symbol of work and harmony, this bee takes on a refined form which adorns the handle of each knife like an aristocratic coat of arms. By projecting the rich heritage of the cutler into the third millennium, Ora-Ïto offers the most magical of French knife brands a new way to leave its mark on everyday life. A sure way to continue to inspire new generations in search of the more human values ​​embodied by Laguiole.</p>
