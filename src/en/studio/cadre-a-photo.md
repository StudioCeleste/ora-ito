<p style="text-align: justify;">With this collection with a divinatory name, Ora-ïto subtly reconnects with antiquity, and does so in the greatest simplicity.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">For this second collection, Ora-ïto has imagined creations with mineral influences. The letter opener has something primitive and recalls a flint eroded by time. The paperweight and the votive have the softness of pebbles polished by the foam of the waves. Elegant, precious and discreet on a desk or on the shelves of a library, the gelular frame in silver metal opts for the softness of memories with its simple lines with rounded corners.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Same sense of simplicity with the square silver metal pocket and the white Corian centerpiece, worked like a gentle tectonic in the confrontation of these two materials rarely combined.</p>
