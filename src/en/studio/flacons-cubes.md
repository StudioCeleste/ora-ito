<strong>A bottle signed Ora ïto</strong>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Designed by Ora ïto, the bottle shakes up the classic codes of perfume design. Reflecting the sensual-playful dimension dear to OKAÏDI, this graphic cube rounds the corners and offers a surprisingly soft touch thanks to the colored silicone. OKAÏDI and Ora ïto, two names that echo each other for a collection of scents placing design at the heart of children's lives.</p>
<strong>A strong-sounding collaboration</strong>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Okaïdi and Ora ïto, beyond a close consonance, share a base of common values. Ora ïto’s work expresses simplicity and its sense of the times. Okaïdi offers creative and modern collections, accessible and respectful of children. Translating this very specific universe, Ora ïto has imagined a cube which rounds the corners, sized to the scale of a child's hand. This graphic shape in silicone material plays on the hollow and raised facets, allowing several bottles to fit together in new geometric shapes. Finally, this cube like no other conceals a technical secret: the vaporizer is integrated inside, erasing the classic codes of bottle design. Childhood is a game and this bottle contains its essence.</p>
