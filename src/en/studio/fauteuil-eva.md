<p style="text-align: justify;">Its organic shape and the opening at the bottom of the backrest take up the spirit of the Evolution armchair (also designed by Ora-Ito for Zanotta in 2007). Eva perfectly illustrates Ora-Ito's work: sober and futuristic lines, a universal and timeless style that can brave time while remaining elegant. The rounded shell is made of integral polyurethane reinforced by a steel insert. Its rounded and ergonomic shapes ensure optimal comfort. The central leg and the swivel base are in varnished steel.</p>
