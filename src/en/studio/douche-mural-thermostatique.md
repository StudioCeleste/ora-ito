<p style="text-align: justify;">Always keen to provide consumers with the best technological advances in balneotherapy, the Spanish contemporary faucet brand Supergrif called on Ora-Ito to rethink the world of the bathroom. For Supergriff, Ora-Ito makes a clean sweep of the precepts that govern the world of traditional taps to offer a new vision of each element making up the collection of taps and showers with refined yet daring ergonomics.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Designed to make the distribution of water in the bathroom ideally simple and harmonious, each of these elements has the same formal nomenclature while having its own proportions and typological appearance adapted to all situations. All the ways of taming water have been redesigned to transform the accomplishment of each gesture. The result? Objects halfway between art and design that renew contemporary aesthetics through a new vocabulary illustrating simplicity.</p>
