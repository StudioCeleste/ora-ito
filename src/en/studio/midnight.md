<p style="text-align: justify;">Convertible corner sofa. With Mid'Night, you'll love being on the corner. As a corner sofa, a lounge area or a very comfortable extra bed, the Mid'Night can be easily transformed while still retaining its exceptional softness. At Dunlopillo, Mid'Night means conviviality.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">By creating a line of “new generation” seats for Dunlopillo, Ora-Ito wanted to invent a modular and easy-to-use collection: sofa, lounge, pouf, convertible devoid of any complex handling. From small to large interiors, models that suit all lifestyles.</p>
