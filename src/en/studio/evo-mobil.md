<p style="text-align: justify;">Ora-ïto did not dream of yet another concept car but of a genetic mutation.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">An unusual creation bringing together more than 400 years of history, a reflection on the evolution of mobility, the legendary know-how of Citroën and a succession of deliberate coincidences. Genetic mutation is an event resulting from copying errors during cell division. When caused by interventions, they become extremely rare. The monumental work created by Ora-ïto is the result of a reflection on the intersection of two typologies, an automobile of the past and an automobile of today, a subtle historical and sociological testimony on the functional mobility of the 16th century merged with the modern world.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">At the border of hypothesis and contemporary art, this hybrid object is based on the fulfillment of a childhood dream with a clear commitment to ecology. We find in the designer's vocabulary, a forward-looking trend or how to project oneself into the future and materialize the imagination. Creation becomes, in essence, a bearer of values, coupled with a playful vision and a breath of empathy, in opposition to the preconceived ideas associated with the world of automobiles, which are often too aggressive.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Inspired by Citroën's first self-supporting chassis and relying on his quality as a designer, Ora-ïto questioned the ergonomics of the interior and the first mobile module. Faithful to his creative approach, he has appropriated the codes, the DNA and the philosophy of this industry to modernize it with existing possibilities and means. Backed by the research and innovation center of the dynastic Citroën, he developed an offbeat, surprising and unexpected sculpture integrating all the parameters and technologies specific to the world of the most avant-garde of French automobile manufacturers, partner on several occasions of the Paris international contemporary art fair (FIAC).</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Called “EVO”, this work qualifies as a monotypic genus, which has only one species. The term “mutation” is used to designate an irreversible modification of genetic and hereditary information. If the mutation reaches the cells then it is transmitted to the descendants of the mutant individual. This change may provide a selective advantage. This is the basis of the process of evolution.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Ora-ïto naturally imagined modules linked to the initial project. A continuity of imagination allowing new actions and uses of the Evo-mobile to be multiplied as options in their own right, these “pluggs” are independent and assemble to the front and rear rods of the vehicle.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">In the case where several genetic mutations come together, we can simply say that they share a common ancestral gene pool. Designed like a sculpture, each pod is a unique and original work of art that combines the dream with the formal, the past with technology. The whole approach lies in the variation of the modules to be plugged, and the diversification of the previously mutated elements. The very idea of ​​working on several entities allows all of the “genetic mutations” to spread a universal message on the evolution of mobility and lays the foundations for new directions never before explored by the automobile industry.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">EVO becomes the spearhead of the artistic lineage: the compatibility of mutation…</p>
