<p style="text-align: justify;">The kitchen utensil kit with base consists of five utensils: large spoon, spaghetti spoon, slotted spoon, frying spatula and ladle, everything used for preparing and serving food.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The utensils are suitable for all types of pans, both in steel and with an internal non-stick coating thanks to the plastic material they are made from, which is scratch-resistant and resistant to high temperatures. <span style="line-height: 1.5em;">The wide base of the structure guarantees very good stability and an innovative system for hanging utensils on the base gives the product an original and truly innovative shape.</span></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">In addition, the particular shape of the handle, also designed to hang utensils on the base, is a guarantee of hygiene because it prevents the utensil from touching the support surface. <span style="line-height: 1.5em;">Ergonomic, with “soft touch” handle, functional, dishwasher safe.</span></p>
