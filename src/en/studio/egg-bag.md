<p style="text-align: justify;">Ora-Ito pays homage to the architect André Bruyère by designing this ovoid bag with organic lines, the beginnings of the Louis Vuitton bag. The design of Egg Bag was inspired by the work of architect André Bruyère, who highlighted the notion of circularity in the 1950s. By rounding the straight and rigid lines that characterize contemporary architecture, André Bruyère restored the nobility of curves and oval figures.</p>
