<p style="text-align: justify;">Ora ïto created this model whose bedside tables are integrated into the bed to create an orange counter-form. This in turn draws a stall to serve as a rest in the manner of a Formula 1 circuit design. A single line builds the structure of this model to meet all the functions that one may need around one's bed.</p>
