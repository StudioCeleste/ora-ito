<p style="text-align: justify;">For AIR’s second album “10000 hz Legend”, Ora ïto designed the French group’s virtual house: The House of Legend. It is a villa entirely dedicated to musical creation and production in an open space suspended between sky and earth. From the architecture to the decoration, everything reflects the ethereal and sophisticated music of AIR to create a very “French Touch” place.</p>
