<p style="text-align: justify;">Faithful to its philosophy, Ora-ïto designs a collection with a very studied simplicity which conceals an extremely elaborate development. Simplexity, a sign of recognition of its universe, is quite evident here.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The cigar humidor and the cigar cutter elegantly interpret this world of enlightened amateurs. Used in full compliance with good practice inside the cellar, the cedar wood is in perfect harmony with the exterior shine of the black lacquer.</p>
