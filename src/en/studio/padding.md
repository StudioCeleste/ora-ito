<p style="text-align: justify;">With a nod to the world of furniture to draw inspiration from its industrialization techniques and an essentially geometric trend in the proportions and relationships between aesthetic elements, PADDING is the work line for men, which plays with the concept of “padded” obtained by high frequency. Padded strips on both sides alternate in the form of micro-grooves which only accentuate the three-dimensionality of the fillings, for an aesthetic result with a clean design but great effect.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The interior organization of spaces, the special compartment for mobile phone, with tab to facilitate its extraction and the large feet designed to ensure the stability of the product and protect it from the supporting surface meet the demands of the modern man, in perpetual movement, attentive to style and elegance. A strong personality for the handle with integrated handle adapter, an element with high aesthetic and functional value.</p>
