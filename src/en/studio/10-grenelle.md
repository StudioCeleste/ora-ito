<b>Ora Ito</b> signs the new offices of <b>LVMH Media Center</b> which houses the<b> Parisian</b> and the<b> Echoes </b>At <b>10 Grenelle </b>in the<b> </b>15th<b> </b>in Paris. A bright pavilion on 4 levels pierced by the movement of a fantastic staircase.
<p class="feat-p">« <i>I wanted this staircase to be the centerpiece of the building. A creature that undulates in space to structure and invade it. A gigantic chimera, half whale, half snake, into which you can rush, which grabs you and spits you out on the right floor. </i>»</p>
A parametric sculpture that catches the eye with its biomorphic structure like a tornado in a deliberately peaceful office environment.

“  <i>The staircase goes beyond the architecture. The offices are deliberately minimal, calm, functional without too many decorative signs. The careful details are there to make you forget. The only surprise for the eye of collaborators and visitors is in this immense body which rises through the building and is diffracted into different functions in several spaces: reception terminal, screens, walls of the canteen and the amphitheater. </i>»

Ora Ito also designed the common areas of the entire 10 Grenelle project, a real estate project which extends over more than 30,000 square meters with its 30 levels including the pavilion dedicated to the LVMH media center.

While the Yooma hotel, built with the complicity of Daniel Buren, opened its doors before the summer and the completely revised architecture of the new Pathé Wepler will soon be revealed to Parisians, Ora Ito confirms an extremely rare 360-degree urban vision through its different scales of intervention.
