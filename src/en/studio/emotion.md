<p style="text-align: justify;">The ultra modular line. Multiple elements, infinitely modular, allowing you to create a designer and contemporary ensemble according to your space and desires. No more space problems with small spaces. E-Motion can do it all: sofa, chaise longue, ottoman, corner sofa bed, single or double bed... And offers all positions, from ground level to low back, high back, or head resting. Easy to adjust, store and therefore move, it is as useful and aesthetic in a bedroom as in a living room, playroom or garden.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Its 17 cm thick high-density foam also offers remarkable comfort in all configurations. Its modern colors allow for an always chic ensemble. At Dunlopillo, E-Motion means “essential”.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">By creating a line of “new generation” seats for Dunlopillo, Ora-Ito wanted to invent a modular and easy-to-use collection: sofa, lounge, pouf, convertible devoid of any complex handling. From small to large interiors, models that suit all lifestyles.</p>
