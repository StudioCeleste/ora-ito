<p style="text-align: justify;">The designer Ora-Ito is visiting Moscow once more so as to prepare something special for Moscow Design Week again. This year, the designer will present the project Café SAVVINSKAYA 23 named after the center of modern design, where it will be located. Its opening is scheduled for November 2012. However, the visitors of MDW have a chance to see the prototype of the bar a month earlier.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The object of art features flexibility of lines and futuristic shapes, so typical of the author. Bright colors magnetize a viewer and do not let him off, causing thoughts about extraterrestrial civilizations and their penetration into our own reality.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">From 11 to 16 of October, Moscow Design Week 2011 proves its status of the most important design event in Russia in its widest sense: from graphic, interior and fashion design to industrial and architectural design.</p>
