<p style="text-align: justify;">The fixed single. D'Night is a transformable product to modulate comfort, sofas vs lounge, or create a space convertible by turning a cushion. Playful with its contrasting edging, it gives a dynamic and current line to your interior. Lightweight, you can change your layout as often as you like.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">By creating a line of “new generation” seats for Dunlopillo, Ora-Ito wanted to invent a modular and easy-to-use collection: sofa, lounge, pouf, convertible devoid of any complex handling. From small to large interiors, models that suit all lifestyles.</p>
