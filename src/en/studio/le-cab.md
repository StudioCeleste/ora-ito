<p style="text-align: justify;">The main characteristic of the space redesigned by Ora ïto is its modernity. The environment is incredibly futuristic and yet the atmosphere is soft and sensual. The radical architecture of the place remains no less logical and rational. The setting created resembles a science fiction movie set with a Kubrickian atmosphere. The lines of force provide astonishing perspectives wherever you are in the club. The DJ booth has been magnified to highlight the guest artist on the decks, making him sacred.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The original atmosphere of this place results from a bold visual and tactile approach through the choice of furniture, materials and colors used. The dance floor was graphically treated like a flat monochrome from which luminous impacts stand out. Horizontal and parallel bands are repeated to form a series of lines on the ground with changing light.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The space seems to be defined by the light which forms a structure highlighting the interior architecture. Mirror games create reflections coloring the entire club at the same time. Light, understood in a playful way, is transformed by automated color changes behind translucent panels and one-way mirrors. These dimly lit halos completely change the atmosphere and general appearance of the place: by switching from one color to another, we have the impression of no longer being in the same place.</p>
