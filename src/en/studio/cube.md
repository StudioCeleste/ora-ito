For its first collaboration with the Italian luxury brand <b>Larusmiani</b>, <b>Ora Ito</b> celebrates the high goldsmithing know-how of<b>Aldo Lorenzi</b>, the newest member of the Larusmiani family.

“ <i>It was natural to turn to one of the best designers of his generation to anchor Aldo Lorenzi in the territory of the contemporary house. A new chapter is opening for Aldo Lorenzi who offers us his know-how. Together we will build a range of domestic tools and accessories for the home of the future whose DNA is the talent of this extraordinary goldsmith. We chose Ora Ito to pass this legacy on to future generations. Its transversal vision of form and functions is unique and totally complementary to our philosophy. </i>» specifies <b>Guglielmo Miani</b>, CEO of Larusmiani.
<p class="feat-p">Ora Ito signs Cube, a volume alphabet made up of 26 shapes/functions each marked with a letter from A to Z.</p>
A fun system that contains a series of trays and containers of different sizes and shapes. Cube is a construction game in space, an infinitely combinable puzzle that allows you to manipulate the spirit and know-how of the Lorenzi house. 26 functions to invent according to your desires: ashtray, vase, jewelry box, trays…

“ <i>This project was born from a double encounter. First with my friend Guglielmo Miani, then with the Aldo Lorenzi legacy, one of the great artisans of luxury in Italy. I wanted to break down the Lorenzi vocabulary to create an alphabet of transmission. A contemporary language with letters carrying tradition. </i>» sums up the designer.

Offered in brass at the Milan furniture fair, Cube is a limited edition of 100 pieces. It will be unveiled in via Monte Napoleone 7, in an installation which will occupy the windows of the historic boutique of the Milanese tailor, specialist in made-to-measure products for 90 years.
