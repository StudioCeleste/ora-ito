<p style="text-align: justify;">The two-in-one lounge single. A daring choice with trapezoidal shapes highlighted by brightly colored piping, for a product with two comforts: sofas or a more relaxed lounge close to the ground. The product can be put in place in the blink of an eye using the seat tilt. Lightweight, Playtime moves throughout your space as you wish.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">By creating a line of “new generation” seats for Dunlopillo, Ora-Ito wanted to invent a modular and easy-to-use collection: sofa, lounge, pouf, convertible devoid of any complex handling. From small to large interiors, models that suit all lifestyles.</p>
