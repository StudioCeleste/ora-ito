<p style="text-align: justify;">The building and its surroundings are designed as a single entity. The message delivered is simple and readable. This unit allows you to understand the area intuitively. The interior and exterior spaces are treated uniformly, so as to offer fluidity and freedom of use. Our bias is a call to dream, an invitation to free your imagination, to create your own story.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The poetry and harmony of the area give it relaxing virtues: the Eden space is a source of well-being. Its pure and simple beauty is an elaborate response to a quest for aesthetics and sincerity. The project creates a “non-architecture”, centered on man and his senses, a sort of regenerating cocoon. This inhabited landscape becomes a shelter, a haven of peace. The arrival is designed to make the visitor naturally slow down; it in fact discovers the area as a whole in sequences. The plant dome constitutes a strong identity signal: it is both respectful of the place and protective. The signage is limited, the totem objects disappear in favor of the sole expression of the offers.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The protected meadow offers picnic areas, games for children, an invigorating course, a hall that can host events or regional markets as well as spaces for contemplation. The landscape elements (merlons and vegetation) are organized in such a way as to conceal parked vehicles and to break the austerity and monotony specific to parking lots. Ground markings in monochrome colors make it easier to spot vehicles and brighten up the whole thing.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The perspectives and routes generated by the play of curvatures actually limit the speed of vehicles, the parking lots wrap around the project. In order to take full advantage of the south orientation for the entrances and terraces, Eden voluntarily turns its back on the highway and its visual and noise pollution. Thus, the entire public area takes full advantage of a clear view of a landscape to contemplate. The impression of fluidity felt outside extends inside: circulation is intuitive. Organized around a tree and a well of natural light, the space evokes a village square. The tree in the center of the restaurant becomes the heart of the new building.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">From the entrance, the circular layout allows optimal visibility of the offers and brands, each distinguished by a distinct shape and color code; they are entities treated independently, their accomplished appearance reinforces the feeling of quality and efficiency. Customers have a free living space open to everyone's use, with several combinations of tables and seat heights possible. A totem equipped with informative screens presents live views of the main destinations via webcam, and information relating to traffic, weather, the region, etc.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">At night, the lighting design creates a warm and reassuring atmosphere: the paths are lit by a series of candelabra, the volume of the building is highlighted by illumination coming from the ground, lighting placed at the foot of the trees highlights the relief of the garden-meadow.</p>
