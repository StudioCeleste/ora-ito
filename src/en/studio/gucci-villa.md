<p style="text-align: justify;">Ora ïto on this virtual project takes up the codes which make up the quintessence of the Gucci style to imagine a house on 3 floors of 300 m2, entirely designed down to the smallest details. A portrait of Marc Jacobs, enthroned on the coffee table, gazes over the living room with its monogram carpet and the bay window which opens completely on one side. The structure of the villa follows a single main line to form an iconic “G”.</p>
