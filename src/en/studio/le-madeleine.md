<p class="feat-p">Red Dot Design Award Winner 2014</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><span style="line-height: 1.5em;">To offer to the Trois-Quartiers </span><span style="line-height: 1.5em;">a lobby</span> worthy of their location at the entrance to the grand boulevards and overlooking the Madeleine church, designer Ora-ïto was tasked with the design of an entrance hall separate from the shops. It was also moved around 16 meters in order to be more visible and entirely dedicated to its reception function. “I wanted the hall to be completely consistent with the exterior facade designed by Laurent Goudchaux and Sébastien Segers,” emphasizes the designer. It must be seen as an extension, an opening onto the street which envelops, which draws in the visitor.”</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Enthroned in the hall, a wooden plant totem, housing in its trunk a lighting system which makes the object almost alive, extends 7 meters high. Before hitting a mirrored ceiling. The game of reflection will give the impression that the trunk is still rising, as if to accompany the staircase and the almost holographic information which will scroll on the wall: temperature, CAC 40 results, traffic jam risks, etc. <span style="line-height: 1.5em;">Enough to restore all its standing to the address which will house, in particular, the Chanel offices.</span></p>
