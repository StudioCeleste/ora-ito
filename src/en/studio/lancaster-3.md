<p style="text-align: justify;">The Lancaster leather goods brand is setting up in the Madeleine and luxury goods district. This very first flagship symbolizes the crucial turning point for the brand which thus establishes its notoriety. To do this, Lancaster called on designer Ora ïto who completely revolutionized retail codes to create a setting entirely dressed in black.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Mineral lair — The challenge was daunting for the energetic Ora ïto. find the architectural identity of the Lancaster leather goods brand. And then, create the showcase which now shows the Lancaster codes. The brand wishes to consolidate its accession to the high-end world. Two years of design, development and then work were necessary for Ora ïto, with his characteristic perfectionism, to be satisfied with this “retail work of art”. Luxury mineral setting of 80m2 spread over two levels at 422, rue St Honoré.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Inspirations — To give body and life to this sculptural and titanic project, the designer drew his inspiration from two sources. The architectural bias was inspired by Soulage, one of Ora ïto’s favorite painters. A tribute to the master, Ora ïto has chosen not THE black but THE blacks restored within this flagship by the mixture of materials. The Staron first, but also the gray mirrors, the matte and textured black laminate (ceramic) on which the leather goods or the soft carpet are placed. Black leather seats highlight the whole. This all-black setting is thus in perfect synergy with the idea of ​​the high-end towards which Lancaster wishes to move today. The brand thus gave carte blanche to Ito who took the time necessary to build this dedicated space. While respecting the brand's specifications, Ito judiciously managed to get Lancaster to surpass himself to let him build this work of art in opposition to the marketing constraints that are too often in place.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">From the oriental origins of the brand's founder, the French designer retained the idea of ​​espalier rice fields, the most famous of which are those of Longji (North-West). He thus built the architecture of the Lancaster space by covering the entire store with layers. These Staron blades (mineral powder composite material developed by Samsung) which run throughout the store, entirely handmade one by one, sometimes move out of alignment to create product presentation areas. The visitor is accompanied upstairs by the continuity of the layers which form the steps of the staircase. The creation of lights – integrated LED strips – beautifully emphasize these lines by creating different tones of black. The effect is astonishing, as rocks contain crystals. A glass banister and a matte black handrail complete the idea of ​​losing your bearings. This minerality recalls one of Ora ïto’s creative principles: organic rationalism.</p>
