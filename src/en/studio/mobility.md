<a href="http://www.ora-ito.com//mobility/" title="Voir la collection Mobility">View the collection</a>

The Gïotto helmet is characterized by its deliberately structured silhouette. On its scale, it echoes the creations of the Ora ïto studio. A flagship piece of the collection, Gïotto mixes machined metal parts with a fabric covering. A tactile encounter that marks the influence of furniture design. Little extra: a docking jack allows you to share your music with your friends.
<ul>
<li>Superior sound quality</li>
<li>Adjustable/adjustable headband</li>
<li>Foldable arch for easy transport</li>
<li>Seamless fabric flat cable</li>
<li>Welcome Jack to share his music</li>
<li>Ergonomic ear cushions for better listening comfort</li>
<li>Built-in microphone for calls</li>
<li>Remote control for line pickup and volume adjustment</li>
</ul>
Speaker diameter: 40mm
Impedance: 32 Ohm
Power: 20mw-30mw
Sensitivity: 120dB +/- 3dB
Frequency response: 20Hz-20KHz
