<p class="feat-p">Cooked by Thierry Marx
Designed by Ora Ito</p>
<b>The combined talent of two big names serving beautiful, good and ethical street food.</b>

It is an original and committed project that Marxito, a restaurant of <i>street food</i> designed and developed by starred chef Thierry Marx and the little prince of design Ora Ito. The alliance of a strong requirement in the quality of everyday food and a permanent concern for a design combining, with simplicity, beauty and functionality. The combination of two talents and a manifesto: eating fast and eating healthy.<span class="Apple-converted-space"> </span>
<b>An original design serving user-friendliness</b>

Lover of Japanese minimalist aesthetics, original and avant-garde designer (he is at the origin of projects as diverse as the Mamo at the Cité Radieuse in Marseille or the offices of the LVMH media division) Ora Ito signs the interior design of Marxito.

And because when it comes to gastronomy everything happens around the table, he designed an endless table, all curvature, descending from the ceiling before transforming into a surface around which the guests sit.<span class="Apple-converted-space"> </span>

A living space where you can eat all day, take a break or check your emails between two appointments, Marxito offers a soft and warm atmosphere, reinforced by light colors and natural materials.
