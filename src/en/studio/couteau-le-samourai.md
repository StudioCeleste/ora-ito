<p style="text-align: justify;">Today, on the initiative of the Forge de Laguiole, the eyes of actor Alain Delon met those of one of the most brilliant designers of his generation: Ora ïto.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">From this meeting was born a unique knife imagined by the designer in the eye of Alain Delon. It was on the occasion of the “Maison &amp; Objet” show in September 2011 and in the “Forge de Laguiole” space specially designed and fitted out under the sign of the man who is undoubtedly one of the symbols of French culture, that as a world premiere, the “Alain Delon” knife was presented to professionals in the Art of Habitat.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">“The Samurai” All the ways to be one… according to Alain Delon and by Ora ïto. Warrior of the Japanese Middle Ages, the Samurai operates alone until the end and with absolute loyalty to his sponsor. On its side, the Katana, a thin and slightly curved saber whose blade ends in a point, often an ornament and above all a symbol of the warrior. Without concession, the samurai draws one of the figures of the absolute masculine embodied by Alain Delon and, definitively, in the cult film of the same name. With this folding knife with an intergalactic appearance, Ora-ïto offers a reinterpretation of the symbolic weapon whose radical modernity was able to seduce the eye of the most famous samurai.</p>
<p class="feat-p">Three definitions, three atmospheres, three faces for this “Samurai” signed Alain Delon made by Master Cutler Virgilio Muñoz Caballero (MOF)</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">A monitor lizard in space... The satin shine of the steel magnifies the strangely gentle inclination of the blade. The shell, also in steel, envelops the cloud gray monitor lizard covering the handle with its cold shine. The bee arises in relief, drawing like a guard for the knife, before coming to curl up, the blade folded, in a wave drawn at the location of the bolsters. The Laguiole codes are evoked and the spirit of the samurai hovers over this strange ship knife, a reptile rocket launched into a futuristic space. The relentless refinement of an exceptional piece.</p>
