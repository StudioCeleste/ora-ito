It was the Marseille designer Ora ïto who was chosen to rethink the metro, the design of which was unveiled this Monday morning by the Metropolis. “ <i>We wanted a timeless metro, capable of crossing the ages, of extreme modernity while preserving the origin. </i>», indicates Ora ïto.

In its original state since its commissioning in 1977, the Marseille metro will finally enter modernity. The 38 trainsets will be fully automated in their entirety by November 2026 with a first delivery in two years.

Alstom will be responsible for designing the trains and integrating them into the current system. A particularly complex project which will not, however, result in traffic stopping for the duration of the work. The first trains will be delivered at the beginning of 2023.
