<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>Ora Ïto champions an Italian stalwart.</strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">As a tribute to the legendary Ico Parisi and his discreet, sensual shapes, French designer Ora Ïto has collaborated with Cassina on the 'Ico' chair, inspired by Parisi's '814' chair, produced in the 1960s for the Italian furniture brand. 'It's 50 per cent my design, 50 per cent Cassina's know-how' says Ïto. 'Thanks to our ongoing exchange, we have created a tribute to 20th century classic design and 21st century modernity.' <em>Cassina.com</em></p>
