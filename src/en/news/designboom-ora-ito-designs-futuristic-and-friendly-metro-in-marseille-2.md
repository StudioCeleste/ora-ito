<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Paris-based designer Ora Ito is working with French rail transport company Alstom to create the upcoming Marseille metro. Carriages feature a minimal white body with blue accents, designed to represent a push towards non-motorized transport.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><em>'The challenge is not speed, but peaceful movement, safety and energy saving,' </em>says Ora Ito. The new trains will consume 25% less thanks to electric braking, led lighting and other optimizations. the installation of the metro is scheduled for 2024. a ‘friendly and futuristic design’ features large windows, an efficient air conditioning system and passenger information systems for which french sound designer Fabien Bourdier has imagined the sound.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The metro will benefit from the latest technological developments in order to increase comfort, availability, accessibility and passenger information. Each train will be made up of four cars and span a length of 65 meters, following a ‘boa’ configuration with open interior gangways that can accommodate up to 500 passengers.</p>
