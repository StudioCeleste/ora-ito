For the versions 2266 and 2266/R legs in natural, wengé-stained or walnut dyed oak. Chromium-plated steel frame. Body in integral polyurethane strengthened with a steel insert, in black or white.

For the version 2266 the body is also available covered with non-removable Eco or leather with seat upholstered with flexible polyurethane.

For the version 2266/R the cover is removable in fabric or leather and Dacron Du Pont, and seat upholstered with flexible polyurethane.

For the version 2269/R, swivel base in varnished steel in black or white. Body in integral polyurethane strengthened with a steel insert in black or white. Removable cover in fabric or leather and Dacron Du Pont, and seat upholstered with flexible polyurethane.
