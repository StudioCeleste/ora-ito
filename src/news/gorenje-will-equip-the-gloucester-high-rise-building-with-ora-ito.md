Henderson Real Estate Agency Limited (“Henderson Land”) has introduced its latest waterfront residence, ‘The Gloucester’. Characterised by a contemporary design aesthetic and an atmosphere of stylish elegance, the new development is furnished with luxurious and innovative kitchen appliances by world-class home appliances brand Gorenje. The developer has also worked with renowned French designer Mr. Ora-Ïto to create a full suite of kitchen appliances that combine stylish appeal and ultra modern functionality to bring residents of ‘The Gloucester’ an exceptional lifestyle experience.

The complete ‘ Futuristic Kitchen’ collection of kitchen appliances and utensils Gorenje Ora-Ïto was on display at Two International Financial Centre to give media in attendance at today’s press conference an exclusive preview of the exciting kitchens at ‘The Gloucester’. Mr. Ora-Ïto flew to Hong Kong as an officiating guest of the press conference where he unveiled the creative concept behind his ‘Futuristic Kitchen’ and introduced the special features of selected appliances.

Designer Mr. Ora-Ïto said, “I am very honoured to participate in the designing of perfect kitchens for ‘The Gloucester’. Situated on the waterfront in the heart of Hong Kong, ‘The Gloucester’ is a prestigious development and the ideal platform for us to showcase our unique offerings. This truly marks a milestone in my design career.”

To enhance the luxurious lifestyle of ‘The Gloucester’ residents, some units are equipped with kitchen appliances of the ‘Gorenje Ora-Ïto Futuristic Kitchen’ series designed by Mr. Ora-Ïto. These include refrigerators, microwaves and hobs. Known for their sleek design and advanced technology as well as for their energy-efficiency and durability, the series’ appliances are the choice of discerning consumers with a passion for life and leisure.

Mr Ora-Ïto, known for his seductive lines, has designed for numerous international brands. The ‘Gorenje Ora-Ïto Futuristic Kitchen’ series embodies his ground-breaking innovative style to set ‘The Gloucester’ worlds apart from other residential developments.
